East Frisian Islands (Germany)

Geographic Overview

The East Frisian Islands are a chain of barrier islands located in the North Sea off the coast of Lower Saxony, Germany. They form a natural protection for the Wadden Sea and are known for their unique coastal landscapes, biodiversity, and tourism significance.
